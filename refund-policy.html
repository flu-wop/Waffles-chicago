Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [date]

Online Orders

Once an online order has been submitted and confirmed, it typically cannot be canceled or modified, as preparation begins shortly after your order is received. If there's an issue with your order — an incorrect item, a quality concern, or a delivery problem — please contact us as soon as possible so we can make it right.

Catering Orders

Catering orders require a minimum notice period for cancellation to receive a full refund. Cancellations made after that window, or on the day of the event, may be subject to a partial charge to cover ingredient and preparation costs already incurred.

Reservations

Reservations can be canceled or changed at no charge up until a set period before your reservation time. Repeated no-shows may affect your ability to book future reservations.

Gift Cards

Gift card purchases are final and non-refundable, except where required by law.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or report an issue with an order, please contact us with your order details. We aim to respond to all refund requests within a few business days.
